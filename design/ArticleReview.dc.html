Should I accept the insurance company's first offer after a Fulton County car accident?

Answer block · 52 words

Usually not. A first offer typically arrives before your treatment is complete, which means it is priced against an injury nobody has finished documenting. Accepting closes the claim permanently. In Georgia you generally have two years to file, and that clock runs regardless of what the adjuster's review schedule looks like.

Why the first offer comes so early

An early offer is not an insult and it is not usually a mistake. It reflects what the carrier can currently document, which after a few weeks is emergency treatment and a police report — and very little else. Anything that develops afterward, including treatment that has not happened yet, is simply not in the number.

The two clocks that are not related

This is the part that catches people. The carrier's internal review has its own timeline, and it is entirely unrelated to Georgia's statute of limitations. Waiting politely for a review to conclude does not pause anything.
